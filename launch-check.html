Why now

October 30 grants change

On October 30, 2026, Supabase enforces a grants change on existing projects: new public tables are no longer auto-exposed to the Data / GraphQL API the way many vibe-coded apps expect. Existing tables keep their current grants; new tables need explicit GRANT. If your RLS and grants were "set it and forget it," this is the week to check. We do not promise your app will break. We help you see what needs a look.

Source: Supabase changelog 45329 (opens in a new tab)

No. This is a read-only config and grants review plus optional fix PRs. Not a pentest, red team, or compliance audit.

No. We check common Supabase/Vercel misconfigs and the Oct-30 grants surface. We do not claim to find every issue or guarantee your app is "secure."

What access do you need?

We only work with the project owner or someone with written permission from the owner (email from the owner's domain or a signed note is fine). You confirm you have that right before we accept any token. We use a read-only scoped credential and revoke it when we deliver. If we learn you weren't authorized, we stop and delete what we received.

Is my app safe after this?

No warranty. This is a limited, read-only review of common misconfigs. You still own security, merges, and deploys. If something is critical, hire a full pentest.

Will you touch production?

No. PRs only. You (or your team) merge and deploy.

What happens to my token and findings?

We delete the credential when we deliver. We do not publish your findings.
